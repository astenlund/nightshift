# Quick wins

Refactors ready to land when time allows; not blocking any feature, but would improve the codebase meaningfully.

This file is **one of four repo-local indexes** agents consult on demand when relevant (alongside `FEATURES.md`, `BUGS.md`, `PATTERNS.md`). Active entries are kept inline, organized under thematic `##` sections you invent as work emerges. When a quick win lands, append a shipped-note entry to [`QUICK_WINS_HISTORY.md`](QUICK_WINS_HISTORY.md); do not move it within this file. Negative-knowledge findings (approaches attempted and reverted) are first-class promotion candidates from the history into the relevant `.claude/patterns/<slug>.md` Cautionary tales sections.

Readiness and graduation are not approval: before spec-governed work, present the current decision-complete digest and obtain explicit agreement in this session.

Backlog prose is one paragraph or one bullet per physical line, never hard-wrapped at a column: a search hit then shows the whole entry, the parsers anchor on whole lines, and an edit shows as one changed line instead of a reflowed block. `/nightshift:ready` reports a hard-wrapped file as a notice and `/nightshift:init-backlog` unwraps it.

Capture shorthand: name the refactor, describe the current smell in a sentence or two, sketch the preferred shape. A reader should be able to start work from the entry alone. Anchor entries on identifiers that survive refactors -- symbol names, entry titles, commit hashes, config keys -- never on line numbers, plan-phase ordinals, bullet positions, or temporal qualifiers ("new", "recent"): a precise locator that rots misleads harder than a coarse one that holds.

**After adding a new entry, run `/nightshift:ready`** from the repo root to confirm it parses as a quick-wins work item against the real grammar in `skills/ready/ready.js`. Quick wins carry neither a `**Requires:**` nor an `**External:**` line; the failure mode to catch is an entry that doesn't parse as a `- ` bullet or `###` heading (ready reports it as a prose-only-section notice) while you can still fix it in the same session.

## Spec review convergence safeguards

- **Gate spec-review fixes by artifact layer before editing.** `internal/revise/SKILL.md` currently routes every skeptic-confirmed finding that fits the edit surface directly to an artifact fix, so a locally plausible request for process supervision, transport framing, timeout machinery, or another implementation choice can inflate a governing spec even when its observable behavior and acceptance boundary were already complete. Observed during the 2026-08-24 to 2026-08-25 deterministic-init-backlog revise-spec run, where later rounds increasingly specified the behavioral harness instead of hardening the approved design. Preferred shape: before preparing any spec mutation, classify the finding as a spec-owned decision or invariant, implementation-plan input, follow-up scope, or invalid finding; only the first class may edit the spec. Route plan inputs to controller-owned scratch for the later implementation plan, route scope expansions to authoritative follow-ups and renewed agreement when required, and record invalid findings through the existing skeptic disposition. The classification is an admission gate before mutation, not another post-fix review.

- **Calibrate revise-spec against implementation-detail escalation.** The completeness and clarity dimensions in `internal/revise/spec.md` ask for complete failure, recovery, lifecycle, and concrete-value treatment without first limiting those demands to decisions the governing spec owns. Reviewers can therefore turn a sufficient behavior-level contract into a detailed implementation design, and each added mechanism creates new surfaces for the next round to inspect. Preferred shape: make reviewers first identify the observable behavior, safety invariant, ownership boundary, durable grammar, irreversible architectural decision, or decidable acceptance criterion that is actually missing. When those are complete and the mechanism is delegated, unspecified implementation choices are plan inputs rather than findings; complementary branches remain required only for conditional decisions the spec owns. Keep this incident-specific calibration separate from the general superpowers-dimension refresh below.

## Agreement digest economy

- **Shorten the decision-complete digest and cut what it costs to produce.** The digest contract in `skills/spec-agreement/SKILL.md` mandates all eleven fields rendered every time "without omitting a material decision for brevity", over a baseline built by reading the full governing set, selecting and hashing each artifact, then rerunning complete resolution and byte capture to prove stability. For a small single-bug scope the render runs longer than the governing spec it summarizes, and the presentation baseline costs two full resolution passes before a single word is shown. Preferred shape: keep decision-completeness as the invariant and attack the rest, for example a density pass on the mandated field prose, collapsing source-empty fields into one line rather than eleven labelled stanzas, and reusing the first pass's artifact bytes for the stability re-check instead of a second cold read. Constraint: no field may become optional and no material decision may be dropped; the two `none explicitly stated` and `none found after full governing-set review` tokens stay distinguishable, since they encode different evidence. Check against [Reuse one resolution-local artifact snapshot across governing-set expansion](#agreement-gate-follow-ups-deferred-during-present-spec-for-agreement-revise-code) first: it already proposes a resolution-local snapshot and may cover the re-read half. Once the digest carries the Operating context field per [Run the operating-context check at shift start, before the agreement digest, and render the section in the digest](#handover-live-claim-surfacing), a multi-artifact governing set renders one section per artifact and they are almost always identical; render an identical section once, listing the artifacts that share it, and render only a diverging artifact's section separately with the divergence named (user ask 2026-09-02).
- **Bundle a composite entry operation in the agreement CLI so callers stop hand-writing driver scripts.** Driving `skills/spec-agreement/spec-agreement.js` for one `lifecycle-entry` today means composing six primitive envelopes (`resolve`, `legacy-detect`, `candidate`, `locate` per artifact, a second `resolve` plus `compare` for the stability check, then `gate`) whose closed shapes are only discoverable by reading the source; the 2026-08-23 run over a one-line quick win needed four structural rejections and an ad-hoc driver script before the first digest could be rendered. Preferred shape: one composite CLI operation (for example `entry`) taking the closed agreement request and returning in one envelope the stable resolution, candidate, current sources with selected bytes, per-artifact `locate` results (line and rendered link), the legacy scan, and the stability comparison, stopping with the same structural errors the primitives raise today. Digest synthesis, response classification, and contract-fit judgment stay in prose; `gate` stays a separate call. Constraint: the composite reuses the primitives rather than duplicating them, and the existing operations remain callable. Evidence (2026-08-23 CI-gate run): a hand-written driver had to reverse-engineer five shapes from source: `selectors` is a one-element array; the target must repeat as the first seed or `resolve` returns a silent `brainstorming-required`; `legacy-detect` takes `{artifacts}`; `compare` takes `{previousCandidate, currentCandidate}`; and every input key order is load-bearing (`exactOrderedKeys`) but unwritten.

## Agreement presentation

- **Present the controller's assessment of the governing text before the digest.** The agreement skill renders the decision-complete digest and asks for agreement as if a written backlog entry were already vetted, but a quick win or feature file is a proposal, not a checked design, and a user reading only the digest can queue work blindly (user idea 2026-09-02, after an assessment surfaced two gaps in an entry that had survived three digest rounds). Preferred shape: before the digest, the controller renders a short assessment of the governing text over the same stable baseline: what holds up, what it would change, accepted costs, and a recommendation, each grounded in the governing text and the repo, so the user reads the digest with the concerns in view. The assessment is advisory and never authority; it stays proportionate (a few bullets, not a review round, since the spec gate remains the full review), and a re-presentation after a requested change re-renders it over the changed entry. On a retained-authority path (exact equality or a validated `within-contract` refresh) no assessment renders, because nothing is being presented.

## Agreement response classification

- **Accept an unambiguous qualified agreement without re-presentation.** The response classifier in `skills/spec-agreement/SKILL.md` maps every conditional assent to `ambiguous`, so "yes, but with medium rigor" stops the run and forces a full re-presentation round even though the adjustment leaves no doubt what the user means (user ruling 2026-09-02). Preferred shape: a fifth response kind, `agree-adjusted`, carrying the ordered adjustment list the user stated. A qualifying adjustment is small and unambiguous: it names one digest field or one settled value and its replacement, with no reading the controller must choose between; a rigor override ("with medium rigor") is the canonical case. The controller applies each qualifying adjustment as a localized design edit to the governing artifact (a rigor override lands as the operating-context deviation entry described in [Run the operating-context check at shift start, before the agreement digest, and render the section in the digest](#handover-live-claim-surfacing); any other adjustment lands in the passage the digest field was extracted from), recomposes the digest from the adjusted baseline, reconstructs the candidate from disk, and binds agreement to the adjusted digest without rendering it again; the presented digest plus the adjustment list is the response evidence, and the controller echoes one line per applied adjustment naming the field or value changed, its new content, and where it landed, so the user verifies the application at a glance without a re-presentation. A response carrying any adjustment that fails the qualifying test classifies as `ambiguous` as today, and the controller asks one clarifying question naming the doubtful adjustment instead of re-presenting the whole digest. Soundness check before applying: the controller judges each adjustment against the governing text and the rigor derivation rule; when it deems one unsound (a tier below what the audience baseline and fired uplifts derive, an exclusion that removes a decision other decisions depend on, and the like), it asks the user to confirm, naming the concrete downsides the user may not have weighed, and applies it only on confirmation; a withdrawn adjustment drops from the list while the remaining ones proceed, and a confirmed one is recorded with its confirmation in the deviation entry or passage it lands in. With no user available to confirm (an unattended continuation), the response cannot arrive at all, since responses are live-session only, so the branch is unreachable rather than undefined. `decideAgreementGate` in `spec-agreement.js` gains the matching closed path: the presented digest, the adjustment list, and the adjusted tuple are all bound, and the reconstruction-equals-pending check runs against the post-adjustment baseline; `spec-agreement.test.js` gains fixtures for the accepted, unsound-confirmed, unsound-withdrawn, and doubtful-adjustment outcomes.

## Release gate follow-ups

- **Let the fetch-depth pin fail honestly on a benign checkout edit.** The conformance assertion in `tests/release-surface.test.js` matches the literal three-line `actions/checkout@v5` block, so bumping the action version or adding another `with:` input above `fetch-depth` fails with `the checkout step must carry fetch-depth: 0` even though it does. Preferred shape: match the `fetch-depth: 0` input independent of the action version and adjacent inputs (a tolerant needle or a small YAML-line scan), keeping the failure message truthful; the assertion stays inside the same conformance test.
- **Name the stale-branch case in the version gate's decrease message.** On a pull request whose branch point predates a version bump on `main`, the range base is `main`'s tip, so `assessVersionSequence` in `tests/release-gate.js` sees the base version above the first branch commit's and `evaluateReleaseGate` reports `the version decreased within the unpushed range at commit 1 of N` instead of the missing-increase message. Never a false pass (`increased` still compares HEAD against the base), so this is message quality: the assessor was written for a linear local range, and the pull-request range is the first case where the base version can exceed what follows. Preferred shape: when only the base-to-first-commit step decreases and no later step does, report that the branch has not out-bumped the base rather than an in-range decrease.
- **Read non-ASCII changed paths verbatim in the version-increase gate.** `git diff --name-only` quotes and octal-escapes a non-ASCII path under the default `core.quotepath=true`, and `classifyShippedBehavior` then rejects the quoted string as not shipped. The tree is ASCII-only today. Preferred shape: pass `-c core.quotepath=false` on the diff call in the live test, or `-z` with a NUL split.

## Review workflow refinements

- **Reserve review authority vocabulary for the controller.** Sweep Nightshift's review surfaces so adjudication, certification, gate evaluation, and stamping name controller actions, while reviewers, skeptics, and verifiers review, test claims, and report evidence without claiming controller authority.

- **Align manual revise fallback with the Workflow path.** Audit the manual fallback against the primary Workflow path and align shared scheduling, skeptic fan-out, adjudication, checkpoint, recovery, and all-inactive wave invariants. - **Use a single-agent review loop outside active Nightshift implementation.** Reserve the shared multi-agent revise engine (`revise-code`, `revise-plan`, and `revise-spec`) for implementing a feature, bug, quick win, or other backlog item through Nightshift, or for a user who explicitly invokes handover or one of those three skills. Every other non-mechanical outgoing diff gets one fresh reviewer per iteration; fold verified critical and important fixes into the diff and dispatch another fresh reviewer until a pass reports neither critical nor important findings, while reporting nonblocking minors under the existing outgoing-review rule. Backlog additions use this lighter path unless the user states that the item will be implemented in the same session, in which case the active Nightshift lifecycle owns its review depth. This rule supersedes the repository's general self-hosting revise-loop default outside active backlog implementation, so implementation updates the matching `AGENTS.md` and README language. Keep [Light revise mode](FEATURES.md#light-revise-mode) distinct by narrowing it to an optional scheduling mode inside an active revise lifecycle rather than creating a second ordinary-change review path. This avoids spending multi-agent time and tokens hardening work that may ship much later or never, and keeps a not-yet-scheduled feature easy to reshape if its context changes or the original design becomes obsolete before implementation.

- **Make review probes hermetic.** Move reviewer and skeptic probes that mutate checkout files into isolated fixtures, or capture and restore the exact original bytes and line endings through guaranteed cleanup, then verify restoration before returning.
