### [Refusal friction](patterns/refusal-friction.md)

The shape most maintainer inbox reports of 2026-10-18 to 2026-11-03 share: the runtime enforces bookkeeping conditions (evidence made stale by an unrelated change, an edit outside a closing scope, a record left open) with the same hard refusal as its provenance and ownership checks, the owner's decision that a thing is settled has no recorded place to land, the lifecycle runs one way, and a refusal often names its condition but not the route out. Raised by the user on 2026-11-03 in Hermes, in their words: "we can't keep working like this. refusals. the 'can't do that now' sort of stuff. i realize we need som checks for provenance, but i've been dealing with a ridiculous amount of this sort of stuff lately. we need to change something." The record maps the seven directions the report offers to the entries and assessment-worklist items that own each, so no feature re-describes the concern, and names the one direction nothing owns yet.
