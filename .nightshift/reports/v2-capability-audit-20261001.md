### Method

For each deleted or modified shipped file, the audit listed every behavior that reached the user: environment variables read, host or terminal integration, writes outside the project, Git or Windows behavior, prompts and choices, presentation, and recovery or safety behavior the user would notice. Pure machinery that v3 replaced by decision (wave scheduler, holistic verifier, tier calculator, agreement digest, dedup judge, checkpoint files, request spool, plan binding) was listed only where it also carried a user-facing capability. Each capability was then checked three ways: presence in the current shipped tree (Present, Partial or Missing, with a citation or the search that established absence), naming by a migration disposition or a backlog, history or report record (quoted, or "none found" with the searches run), and coverage by the 2026-09-21 audit, whose stated method enumerated from the twelve shipped feature-history entries (`pre-v3-shipped-capability-audit-20260921.md:9` and `:19`), so capabilities that shipped as quick wins or inside skill text without a feature record were outside it. Line links were used only to calibrate what a finding looks like and are not reported.

One structural observation explains most findings: v2's handover lifecycle text (`skills/handover/SKILL.md` at `8ca3cb4^`, 146 lines) and the review profiles (`internal/revise/{code,plan,spec}.md`) carried many small operational rules that no feature record owned. The v3 operating brief replaced them with shorter principles, and the migration ledger assessed backlog entries rather than skill text line by line (`V3-MIGRATION.md:9` to `:13`), so rules that lived only in skill text had nothing to be named by.

#### Finding 1: the live-claim marker protocol in governing specs

What it did. The spec review's Design-soundness criterion made a reviewer mark any claim the repository cannot settle (a third-party service, browser, OS or host behavior) with a literal probe bullet ending `(live-claim: provisional)` or `(live-claim: provisional, awaiting <precondition>)`, so that downstream tooling could find it (`internal/revise/spec.md:73 at 8ca3cb4^`). Handover's spec gate probed every such marker against the live system in every distinct context, rewrote a confirmed one to `(live-claim: probed <date>)`, split a contradicting probe three ways, and carried an unprobeable one forward as a morning-report item (`skills/handover/SKILL.md:109 at 8ca3cb4^`); the validation step reported surviving markers as flags or notes, a bare `provisional` marker downgraded a clean detection to confirm-first (`:57`), the end-to-end step rewrote markers it settled (`:134`), and the morning report recorded each disposition in the marker itself, `probed` or `deferred <date>`, "so the ruling survives the conversation and the completion record is never written over silently-unprobed claims" (`:146`). The user's own spec files carry these markers: for example `.nightshift/features/second-opinion-gates.md:29` carries `(live-claim: provisional, awaiting ...)` and `.nightshift/features/present-spec-for-agreement.md:330` to `:343` carry `(live-claim: deferred 2026-08-19)` lines. Introduced by `3ff210a` (2026-08-02, "feat(handover): dialog triage and live-claim verification gate") and `447654f` (same day, "feat(revise): live-claim detector and proportionality guards"); refined through `3cd3e5c`, `87a7c73`, `4bb64ae` and `702d8dd` (2026-08-20 to 2026-09-03).

v3 status: Missing. `rg -i live-claim` over the current shipped and guidance surfaces returns nothing (`sweeps/live-claim.current.txt`, 0 lines; a direct grep of `internal/workflow.md`, `internal/runtime/REFERENCE.md`, `internal/runtime/review.js`, `README.md`, `WORKFLOW.md`, `VISION.md` for `live-claim|live claim|provisional` also returns nothing). The current spec dimension "Soundness and integration: feasibility in the real project and operating environment" (`internal/workflow.md:52`) does not ask for a marker, and nothing reads the markers still present in the user's records.

Naming: none found as a disposition. The 49 record hits (`sweeps/live-claim.records.txt`) are: three shipped quick-win history entries describing the v2 behavior as delivered (`QUICK_WINS_HISTORY.md:18`, `:31`, `:41`), the markers and stamp-suffix lines inside v2 feature records, two verification-paragraph uses in `.nightshift/specs/`, and the evidence-inventory filename `live-claims.json` in a report. No row of `V3-MIGRATION.md` or `MIGRATION_STATUS.md` contains the term. Suggested disposition (suggestion only): decide between restore (fold the marker grammar and the probe-rewrite rule into `live-boundary-verification.md` as the origin of its pending marker, so the v2 form is reused rather than reinvented) and record as retired (add the v2 protocol to that record's history and leave the existing markers as inert history).
