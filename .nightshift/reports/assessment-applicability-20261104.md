Lead assessments survive canonical changes during dispatch and before import with their original attribution, snapshots, context and commitments. Their private source copy still has to match its assignment. Fresh lead originals may retain source in a bounded shared cache for later independent relevance; other dispatches retain no full source copy. The full original assessment stays in dispatch artifacts, while worker and decision state keep a compact binding. Eviction or unavailable caching leaves future source-dependent relevance pending and does not erase an original assessment. Status reports input and commitment differences for completed and open tasks, including changes made without a repair transition, without writing state. Recorded dispatch order keeps a late-imported older result from displacing newer coverage or reopening completed work without new findings; legacy records without complete dispatch history retain their existing ordering.

A resumed originating reviewer or independent replacement can preserve applicability for individual covered tasks on exact later inputs and commitments, require reassessment or leave it unresolved. The decision is stored separately from the assessment, uses the same lead report schema as ordinary review, and cannot become fresh assessment, check evidence or finding closure. Gates, shared task coverage, closing records and historical discharge distinguish these records. Skeptic validation and dialogue evidence bind to accepted commitments; import, disposition and repair refuse stale authority after a material commitment change, and status exposes `validationCommitmentsCurrent`. Legacy evidence needs a valid surviving binding or the documented unamended-commitment fallback. A reassess or unresolved answer survives input drift as an obligation until a later independent preserve settles it, a new fresh assessment establishes a new basis, or exact original reversion restores coverage. A later preserve settles the earlier negative answer without approving subsequent edits; those edits need ordinary applicability comparison. Matching paths alone do not preserve a decision. Original input and commitment reversion restores coverage only when the relevant context also matches: specs bind declared deciding inputs, while code and docs retain their project-context binding.

Existing intermediate stage barriers and the legacy spec-readiness prerequisite remain: dependent engineering and final completion still require a directly fresh acceptable original spec assessment, even when a relevance decision preserves the spec task's own applicability. Finding validation and dialogue freshness, probe currency, closing baselines and required findings/checks also remain. The final exception-approval mechanism, publication choice and completion stamp are future work. The assessment worklist remains open; this candidate is not the complete conditions-based workflow.
