## Session retrospective

The campaign demonstrated the value of independent review while exposing substantial orchestration cost. A strong model's draft still introduced unintended constraints, and a fresh reviewer found them while the draft was available to the user. The collection failure also reinforced a specific lesson: a moment with no registered workers does not establish that a host has finished processing their notifications. Process cleanup, native results, workflow completion and accounting are separate facts. The final stop case also showed why skeptical validation matters even for minor findings: it confirmed the real convention slip and rejected a second, incorrectly cited violation.

The confirmed production defects were repaired at their source and reassessed. The private collection defect is retained as a reuse restriction, not promoted into a new product feature or a false product failure. Existing instructions already require observable completion, preservation and honest uncertainty; no additional global instruction proposal is warranted from this session.
