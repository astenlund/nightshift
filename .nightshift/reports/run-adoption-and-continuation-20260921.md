## Implemented behavior

The runtime can adopt the current unfinished run under explicit user authority. A stopped source can keep its original chat open. An abandoned running source needs its exact claimed native process to be confirmed ended. Active or uncertain work prevents takeover. The transfer preserves the run, commitments, evidence, limits and reports, records ownership history, and leaves the adopter stopped and attended until reconciliation and explicit resumption.

Current execution resources are separate from creation provenance. Retained adoption requires the same store and exact compatible release, protects the target reference before transfer and reconciles interrupted attachment afterward. Historical source references cease blocking retirement only after the target is protected. Older retained releases without adoption support are not upgraded automatically.

Engineering needs a current native controller claim. Checks and private probes reserve execution before launch, and pending named checks cannot expose an older pass after interruption. Review selection excludes former controllers, while valid imported historical assessments retain their original attribution. Report bookkeeping remains available to the current owner after a process change without granting engineering authority.

Continuation observations are scoped to their controller and run. Resumption invalidates old verification; explicit invalidation preserves the handover and its report duties. A functioning Stop hook protects recorded handed-over work after an attended downgrade, with user stops, limits and decision pauses taking precedence. Claim refreshes and repeated negative observations cannot reset the no-progress reminder bound.
