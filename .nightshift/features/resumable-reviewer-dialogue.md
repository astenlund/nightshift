# Resumable reviewer and adversarial repair dialogue

Delivered in the local 3.3.0 candidate on 2026-10-01 in run `edb0199e-f5f3-4fab-a55f-8fa909a08289`, under the [governing spec](../specs/resumable-reviewer-dialogue.md) the user accepted that day, which settles the questions listed under Before implementation below. The [acceptance report](../reports/resumable-reviewer-dialogue-20261001.md) records the review history, the deterministic evidence and the installed-host campaign on Claude Code and Codex. Current behavior below describes the state before delivery.

## Origin

Raised by the user on 2026-09-29, outside any run, first as a question: "quick investigation: before the v3 migration, we had a planned feature that described an adversarial conversation between a skeptic and a resumable reviewer. what happened to that? i can't find it in the backlog."

After the investigation below, the user chose to track it as its own feature, in their words: "this would be a valuable improvement; i've previously seen strong evidence that continuity can be very helpful when repairing brittle parts of the code. a fresh reviewer might miss something that the first one was a bit lucky to find. also. we'd have to re-evaluate if we want a direct exchange or not between the reviewer and the skeptic, as the controller makes all go/no-go decisions for fixes today. should responsibility move to the skeptic for small fixes, only escalating when a fix would require a bigger change?"

## History

The v2 design [Adversarial repair dialogue](adversarial-repair-dialogue.md), now a design archive that no active entry tracks, had the controller resume the skeptic and the originating reviewer to assess each admitted finding's consequence and the available responses. The two stayed adversarial throughout: the reviewer explained the consequence of leaving a finding unresolved while the skeptic challenged its evidence, likelihood and significance, and when a repair was warranted the skeptic proposed and revised it while the reviewer checked closure, regressions, ambiguity and adjacent invariants, with neither editing the artifact.

The v3 migration simplified it on 2026-09-06, in [its disposition](../../V3-MIGRATION.md#finding-decisions): "Keep skeptic validation of every finding, separate authority and value judgments, and controller-owned implement/defer/skip decisions. Use existing reviewer and skeptic evidence for straightforward decisions; extend dialogue when uncertainty or disagreement remains." [The migration status](../MIGRATION_STATUS.md) recorded it as Present, with "The supported replacement review path exists", until the migration accounting audit of 2026-09-29 corrected it to Partial with this entry as its tracked destination.

## Current behavior

[The operating brief](../../internal/workflow.md) and [revise-code](../../skills/revise-code/SKILL.md) keep the disposition's core: every finding receives fresh skeptic validation against concrete evidence, factual validity, authority and practical value stay distinct, and each finding is decided as implement, defer, skip or refuted. Reviewers and skeptics cannot edit reviewed inputs, and every repair batch needs a cumulative strong broad assessment. [WORKFLOW.md](../../WORKFLOW.md) says the controller decides whether to implement, defer or skip, and that where consequences, value or repair quality remain unclear or disputed, "those agents continue the adversarial dialogue"; [VISION.md](../../VISION.md) likewise says to extend the adversarial dialogue when consequence, likelihood, value or repair quality remains unclear or disputed. A search on 2026-09-29 found no mention of a dialogue, a dispute or resuming a reviewer in the brief, the skills or [the runtime reference](../../internal/runtime/REFERENCE.md#independent-assessment).

The runtime cannot resume a reviewer. Every review or skeptic dispatch starts a new session: `validateRequest` in `internal/runtime/review.js` admits only `host`, `model` and `effort` in each candidate, and the dispatch passes no session to the host runner. The Claude runner in `internal/runtime/hosts.js` has a `--resume` option that the dispatch never uses, and the Codex runner has none.

The user's own global review-loop routine, outside the plugin, already retains one reviewer per category across repair rounds and dispatches a replacement, given the full cumulative change and the prior findings, for one that cannot resume. On 2026-10-01, for the reason recorded under Settled questions, the user added a final fresh review to that routine and to the user's push gate, with the user's own rules: a fix is always verified by resuming the reviewer that raised the finding; every clean result (no critical or important findings) from a resumed reviewer is followed by a fresh-context reviewer; and endless loops are prevented by passing acknowledgements to the reviewers and by the controller deferring, at its discretion, the minor findings of a fresh-context reviewer that returns only minor ones. The user also accepted the controller's refinements: a clean result counts only findings that survive verification; a replacement reviewer is treated like a resumed one; a fresh-context reviewer gets no earlier findings beyond the acknowledgements; a finding verified false becomes an acknowledgement; acknowledgements state settled decisions and never the author's reasoning; and deferred and refuted findings are reported with the loop's result. The user holds Nightshift to a higher standard than this routine ("i've been holding nightshift to a higher quality standard"), so the routine is a floor, not a ceiling, for this entry's spec.

## Direction

- Keep the reviewer that found an issue available through its repair, resuming it rather than always starting fresh, because continuity helps when repairing brittle code and a fresh reviewer might miss what the first one found partly by luck.
- Restore the escalation the v3 disposition kept: extend the dialogue when consequence, value or repair quality remains unclear or disputed.
- Restore the repair reasoning the same disposition kept, which the migration accounting audit of 2026-09-29 found missing: "Require enough repair reasoning to judge the approach without a complete patch proposal for every finding." Today no instruction or report field asks for it before a repair is applied, so a repair's approach is judged only afterwards, by the cumulative reassessment.

## Settled questions

The user agreed these answers on 2026-10-01, when the entry graduated from Exploring to current work, so that [Dispatch reviewer peers and return their evidence to the lead](reviewer-peer-dispatch.md) could graduate behind it. Each question is kept with its answer, and parts that were not put to the user are marked as such.

- Whether the reviewer and the skeptic exchange directly or only through the controller, given that the controller makes every go/no-go decision on fixes today. Settled: only through the controller, which relays between the resumed reviewer and the skeptic when consequence, value or repair quality stays unclear or disputed, and keeps every go/no-go.
- Whether responsibility for small fixes moves to the skeptic, escalating to the controller only when a fix would need a bigger change; how small is judged; and how that fits the skeptic's independence and the rule that reviewers and skeptics cannot edit reviewed inputs. The v2 design kept edits away from both, with the skeptic proposing a repair and the reviewer checking its closure. Settled: the controller keeps every fix decision. The skeptic proposes a repair with enough reasoning to judge the approach, the controller decides and applies it, and the resumed reviewer checks its closure; neither agent edits reviewed inputs, so no size threshold is needed.
- How continuity coexists with the fresh reviews the brief requires: whether a resumed reviewer can supply the cumulative strong broad assessment after a repair batch, or verifies its own findings' repairs with a fresh assessment still owed, and whether skeptic validation stays fresh. Settled as both, in the user's words: "when the resumed agent returns lgtm at the end of a review loop, do one final review with a fresh agent". During the loop the resumed reviewer verifies each repair batch and reassesses the full cumulative change, which counts as the strong cumulative assessment; once it returns clean, one final review by a fresh-context reviewer follows before the loop ends. Skeptic validation of each new finding stays fresh. On 2026-10-01 the user confirmed that reversing the agreed rule named under Before implementation is intended ("i did") and gave the reason, in their words: "a resumed reviewer -- especially one that's been through a few rounds -- might get a bit of tunnel vision and might therefore miss other issues. a resumable reviewer is good for reviewing fixes for an issues it originally found, but the results should be verified with fresh eyes afterwards."
- Runtime support on both hosts: resuming a session through dispatch, how a resumed session's receipt is attributed and checked for fresh inputs, the context limits of a long-lived reviewer, and the fallback when a session cannot be resumed. Settled for the fallback, the part put to the user: a reviewer that cannot be resumed, for a host limit, an exhausted context or a lost session, gets a replacement given the full cumulative change, the prior findings and which repairs remain unverified, and resumed receipts keep the same reviewer's attribution and are still checked for fresh inputs. How dispatch resumes a session on each host and how a long-lived reviewer's context limits are handled were not asked at triage and are left to the spec. Whether a replacement's clean verdict triggers the final fresh review was not asked either, but the user's global routine, which this entry's spec may not fall below, treats a replacement like a resumed reviewer, so the spec does at least the same.
- Relations: [Initial reviewer selection](initial-reviewer-selection.md) already says subsequent passes can resume the initial lead, and [Background review and assessment of selected work](selection-review-and-assessment.md) adds agents whose findings would face the same question. Not asked at triage; noted here that neither depends on this entry, nor it on them.

## Before implementation

The change adds session resumption to review dispatch on both hosts, a final fresh review to the review loop, and repair reasoning and closure checks to the brief and the reviewer and skeptic prompts. The final fresh review reverses, for review loops with a resumed or replacement reviewer, the agreed rule that a separate final reviewer is not mandatory, which [WORKFLOW.md](../../WORKFLOW.md), [the v3 feature](nightshift-v3.md) and [the operating brief](../../internal/workflow.md) state; the work amends every statement of that rule, found by a sweep by claim that includes the revise and handover skills, and the spec settles what follows when the final fresh review raises findings, within the floor the user's routine sets (see Current behavior). It needs a concise governing spec, a plugin version increase, and a decision at the start of the work, for each host, between a budgeted installed-host check and deterministic evidence only with the model-owned behavior marked unverified. Tracking and readiness do not authorize implementation.
