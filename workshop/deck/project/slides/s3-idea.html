Step 3 · The idea

Autonomy bought with trust is not reliability

Stop fires after every reply: a finished task, a plain answer, a question only you can answer. A guard that blocks a question forces Claude to guess. The run should not wait for you without need, and it should not lower its standard to avoid waiting.

Guard only what was handed over

While you are at the keyboard the guard stays quiet. Only you decide that the work is handed over.

Park decisions, keep working

Claude records the question, sets that task aside and carries on with everything that does not depend on it.

Why I chose this for Nightshift

Nightshift's Stop hook follows both rules, because I want to come back in the morning to work that is either done or clearly waiting on me, never guessed.
