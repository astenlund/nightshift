Do Claude models actually stop early?

Less than you might expect: "Work through TASKS.md." finished everything in one turn. In rehearsal Claude stopped:

At the end of the scope you named, to report back (step 1).

At a decision only you can make, again and again (step 2).

When an error ended the turn, such as compaction thrashing.

To ask. A reply ends the turn; the dialog holds it open.

Building Nightshift, I found that Codex models tend to end their turn early and Claude models mostly don't, so on Claude Code the Stop hook is a safety net rather than the engine.

The goal docs list usage limits, exhausted credits and failed authentication as other errors that end a turn. The guard earns its keep on long runs, after compaction, when a prompt is ambiguous about scope, and at 3 a.m. when nobody is there to type "carry on".
