Step 4 · The idea

The summary is not the record

Compaction replaces the conversation with a summary. Keep the record outside the conversation, and put a short brief of what is owed back in front of Claude right after compaction.

Comes back from disk

Project-root CLAUDE.md. Up to five files Claude read or edited with its own file tools; over 5,000 tokens, only the path.

Goes through the summary

Everything else, including instructions you gave only in conversation and context that hooks added earlier.

SessionStart with matcher compact runs after /compact and after automatic compaction; its additionalContext lands in Claude's context.

Why I chose this for Nightshift

After every compaction, Nightshift's hook hands Claude a short brief of what is still owed and has it check that against the files first.

Stop Guard Cook-Along · Step 4 · Source: Claude Code docs, "What survives compaction"

Both triggers were confirmed in rehearsal. The brief should tell Claude to check the record against the files, because a task can be half done. For Nightshift: a run I hand over at midnight can compact several times before morning, and I don't want its obligations to depend on how well each summary turned out.
