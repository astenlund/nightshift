Step 6 · Questions

Couldn't Claude just tick the boxes, or edit the hook?

Tick the boxes: it could

The guard checks the record, not the work. In rehearsal Claude refused: "ticking it would record a sign-off that nobody gave". The pressure peaks when ticking is the only way out.

Edit the hook: harder

.claude/ is a protected path. Claude must ask you in the default and accept-edits modes, a classifier decides in auto mode, and only bypassPermissions skips the check.

That is why, in Nightshift, the agent never edits the record directly, and nothing counts as done until an independent reviewer has looked at it.
