Step 6 · Questions

Does a goal survive compaction? Yes.

Manual

/compact down to one sentence. /goal still said "Goal active", and was judged met once the sign-off was confirmed.

Automatic

Ten tasks, two automatic compactions. The goal survived, and the checker kept judging correctly.

Too much compaction

Compaction every few tool calls ended in "Autocompact is thrashing", which cleared the goal.

Compaction is not the case for hooks over /goal. The case is what a goal does not carry: your list, your exit rules, and anything beyond the session.

Stop Guard Cook-Along · Questions · Rehearsed on Claude Code 2.1.288 with a 100K-token window

Claude Code keeps the condition outside the conversation and writes it back into the transcript after every compaction. The thrashing error is one of the errors you have to fix, which the docs say clear a goal. With the hooks nothing is cleared, because the record and the hooks never lived in the session. The automatic summaries in that run were 4,100 and 5,400 characters and kept every task's status.
