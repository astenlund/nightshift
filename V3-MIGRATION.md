## Agreed MVP policy

Feature loss is acceptable where carrying a capability forward requires a substantial rebuild. Distinguish capabilities that are retired, valuable capabilities whose rebuild is deferred, and reduced capabilities whose useful subset ships with stated limits. A mode that cannot work reliably without a missing capability remains unavailable until its supporting pieces exist. Choose the MVP around a dependable unattended path with the required quality assurance. Feature loss may remove advanced capabilities, but must not leave normal handover dependent on routine mid-run intervention or user code inspection. The MVP must describe its actual boundaries and preserve the commitments it does make.

The ordinary closing report and retained follow-up triage remain the baseline. The manager proposal's separate external-delivery transaction does not become a completion prerequisite.
